# Code of conduct

Purify is a small project with a high-trust mission. We welcome people of every experience level and background.

We expect participants to be respectful, specific and patient. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualised language, and deliberately unsafe advice are not acceptable.

If a conversation becomes difficult, pause and move it back to the user problem, the evidence and the safety boundary. Maintainers may edit, close or remove contributions that do not meet these standards.

To report a concern, contact the maintainers privately through [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting form](https://github.com/Mahdi-mortazavi/purify/security/advisories/new) or ask for a private channel in an issue without sharing sensitive details.
